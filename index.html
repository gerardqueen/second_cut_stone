How pricing works

Simple, upfront pricing

Each offcut has its own price per square metre. You pay for the area of the piece you need, plus each finished edge by the metre. Cut edges that sit against a wall or cabinet don't need finishing.

Stone

Area (m²) × the offcut's £/m². Round and L-shaped pieces are priced on the rectangle they're cut from.

Edges

Length of each finished edge (m) × that finish's £/m.
